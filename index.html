Research

My research interest lies in 3D Computer Vision, particularly including human-object interaction, human motion recovery, neural rendering, 3D reconstruction, as well as their applications in mixed reality and robotics.

AlignHOI: Hand–Object Reconstruction via Alignment and Refinement
Liting Wen, Xin Lv, Jungbin Cho, László A. Jeni, Xiao-Xiao Long
under review
arXiv /

Reconstructing hand–object interactions from monocular videos by combining coarse alignment with optimization-based refinement for both hand and object.
OnlineHMR: Video-based Online World-Grounded Human Mesh Recovery
Yiwen Zhao, Ce Zheng, Yufu Wang, Daniel Yang, Liting Wen, László A. Jeni
under review
arXiv /

An online framework that recovers 3D human meshes and world-grounded camera trajectory from streaming video without using future frames.
Sketch-1-to-3: One Single Sketch to 3D Detailed Face Reconstruction
Liting Wen, Zimo Yang, Xianlin Zhang, Chi Ding, Mingdao Wang, Xueming Li
ACM Multimedia Asia, 2025   (Oral Presentation)
arXiv /

An approach enables high-fidelity 3D face reconstruction from just one hand-drawn sketch, achieving accurate geometry and expressive details.
FreeDance: Towards Harmonic Free-Number Group Dance Generation via a Unified Framework
Yiwen Zhao, Yang Wang, Liting Wen, Hengyuan Zhang, Xingqun Qi
ICCV, 2025
project page / paper / code

A unified framework that generates group dances of free-number dancers, harmonically aligned with music while ensuring motion diversity and cross-dancer coordination.
MAVERIX: Multimodal Audio-Visual Evaluation and Recognition IndeX
Louise Liuyue Xie, Avik Kuthiala, George Zhihong Wei, Ce Zheng, Ananya Bal, Mosam Dabhi, Liting Wen, Taru Rustagi, Ethan Lai, Sushil Khyalia, Rohan Choudhury, Morteza Ziyadi, Xu Zhang, Hao Yang, László A. Jeni
AAAI, 2026
project page / paper

A benchmark that evaluates multimodal large language models on real-world audio-visual understanding, highlighting the gap between current models and human comprehension in integrating sound, vision, and context.
A Secure and Efficient Data Transmission Method With Multilevel Concealment Function Based on Chaotic Compressive Sensing
Wei Wu, Haipeng Peng, Liting Wen, Yuhong Liu, Fenghua Tong, Lixiang Li
IEEE Sensors Journal, 2023
paper /

A secure, efficient data transmission method with multilevel privacy protection based on chaotic compressive sensing, tailored for resource-limited IoT sensors.
