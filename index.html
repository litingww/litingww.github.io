Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of 3D/4D computer vision & robot learning. I want to build intelligent systems that can understand and interact with the physical world.

  • 👀Perceiving the physical world: How do humans interact with objects (AnchorHOI)? How do humans move and behave in the world (OnlineHMR, FreeDance)? Can we recover detailed 3D structure from sparse observations (Sketch-1-to-3)?
  • 🦾Learning to act: How can robots learn diverse manipulation skills that generalize to any object? What visual representations matter for action? I've been working on reinforcement learning for general-purpose robotic manipulation!
